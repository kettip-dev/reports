## 3. Order Channel Dynamics (M-App vs. C-App)

The order channel usage shows a distinct 3-phase operational evolution:

```
[Phase 1: June 15 - June 19]  ==>  Merchant App Dominant (80-90% M-App)
[Phase 2: June 20 - July 08]   ==>  Customer App Transition (100% C-App)
[Phase 3: July 09 - July 22]   ==>  Merchant App Reversion (100% M-App)
```

### Channel Comparison Table

| Channel | Order Volume | Share (%) | Revenue (USD) | Revenue (KHR) | Avg Order Value (AOV) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **M-App (Merchant App)** | 498 | 47.25% | $1,006.40 USD | 4,025,600 KHR | $2.02 USD (8,080 KHR) |
| **C-App (Customer App)** | 556 | 52.75% | $1,131.77 USD | 4,527,080 KHR | $2.04 USD (8,160 KHR) |

### Strategic Observations
1. **Initial Store Onboarding (Phase 1: Mid June)**:
   - High volume of manual/walk-in or merchant-assisted orders processed through **M-App** (up to 32 orders/day).
2. **Online Customer Adoption (Phase 2: Mid June – Early July)**:
   - Rapid migration to **C-App** between June 20 and July 08, reaching **100% consumer-app ordering**. Peak daily order volume hit **44 orders ($89.76 USD / 359,040 KHR)** on June 20.
3. **Operational Shift (Phase 3: Mid July Onward)**:
   - Starting July 10, 2026, 100% of orders shifted back to **M-App**. This suggests either POS dispatch routing changes or direct dark-store order fulfillment mode.

## 5. Key Insights & Actionable Recommendations

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Actionable Recommendations for Operations & Merchant Account Management:**

1. **Investigate Channel Shift on July 9–10**:
   - Prior to July 9, C-App generated 100% of customer orders. After July 10, C-App dropped to 0% while M-App jumped to 100%. Verify if store listing status, customer app visibility, or merchant POS app mode was accidentally modified.
2. **Promote Combo Packs & Item Upselling**:
   - The current Average Order Value (AOV) sits at **$2.03 USD (8,120 KHR)**. Introducing bundled menu items (e.g., Duck + Green Mango Sour Set) targetting $3.50 – $5.00 USD could significantly boost total GMV without requiring additional order volume.
3. **Weekend Demand Optimization**:
   - Peak sales consistently occur on Fridays and Saturdays (e.g., June 20 with 44 orders, July 17 with 37 orders). Ensure dedicated rider allocation during 11:00 AM – 2:00 PM and 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM peak hours.
